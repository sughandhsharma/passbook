Refund & Cancellation Policy

Pass-book charges two small, one-time platform fees: a listing fee to publish an item, and an unlock fee to view a seller's contact. This page explains when these are and aren't refundable.

1. Listing fee

The listing fee is charged to publish your item on the ledger. It is non-refundable once your listing has been successfully published, since the service — making your item visible to buyers — has been delivered at that point. This is true even if the item does not end up selling.

2. Unlock fee

The unlock fee is charged to reveal a seller's contact details. It is non-refundable once the contact details have been shown to you, since that is the service being paid for. Pass-book is not able to guarantee that a seller will respond, that the item is still available, or that a sale will be completed — these happen directly between users, outside the platform.

In short: both fees pay for a platform action (publishing a listing, or revealing a contact) — not for the outcome of a sale. Once that action is completed, the fee is considered earned.

3. When a refund IS available

If you were charged but the platform failed to deliver the service you paid for — for example, your payment succeeded but your listing never got published, or you paid to unlock a contact but never received it due to a technical error — you are entitled to a refund. Contact us with your Razorpay payment ID and we will investigate and refund verified cases.

4. How to request a refund

Email sughandhsharma9@gmail.com with your registered email, the payment ID from Razorpay (shown in any payment confirmation or error message), and a description of what happened. We aim to respond within 3–5 business days.

5. Cancellations

Since both platform actions (publishing, unlocking) happen instantly on successful payment, there is no cancellation window after payment succeeds. If you cancel or close the payment popup before completing payment, you are not charged.
