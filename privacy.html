Privacy Policy

What Hando collects, why, who sees it, and how you can control or delete it.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short
  • We collect your email, the listing details you enter, your contact number and payment records.
  • Your contact number is shown only to the one buyer who pays to unlock it.
  • Payments go through Razorpay; we never see your card or UPI details.
  • We do not sell your data or show ads. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

1.Who we are

Hando is operated by Sughandh Kumar Sharma, a sole proprietor based in Delhi, India. For this policy, Hando is the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). This page explains what personal data we collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: sughandhsharma9@gmail.com.

2.What we collect

WhatWhen
Account details: email address and password (stored securely, hashed, by Firebase Authentication)When you sign up or log in

We do not collect your precise location, contacts list, or files from your phone. The photo you add is the one you choose to upload.

3.Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To show your listing, run the unlock and 5-minute payment hold, and give your contact to the buyer who paid.
  • To take, verify and reconcile payments and to handle refunds and disputes.
  • To keep Hando safe: preventing fraud, spam and misuse, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To reply to you, and to meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not show advertising. We do not send you marketing messages unless you ask for them.

4.Your consent and legal basis

We process your data with your consent, which you give when you create an account and use Hando, and where the law permits it for legitimate uses such as completing a payment you made or meeting a legal requirement. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 8), though that may stop you from using Hando.

5.Who can see your data

  • Other users: the details on your listing (other than your contact number) are visible to people browsing Hando. Your contact number is shown only to the one buyer who pays to unlock it. That buyer sees it on their device afterwards.
  • Service providers who help us run Hando: Google Firebase (login and database), Vercel (hosting and photo storage) and Razorpay (payments). They process data on our behalf and under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect users, such as in response to a valid legal request.

Our providers may store or process data on servers outside India. We choose providers with recognised security practices.

6.Storage on your device

Hando uses your browser or app storage (local storage) to keep you logged in and to remember the contacts you have unlocked, so they stay available on your device after the listing is removed. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If you clear your browser or app data, or uninstall the app, the saved unlocked contacts on that device are lost, so note down what you need.

7.How long we keep data

  • Listings are removed when the seller marks them sold or a buyer unlocks them.
  • Account data is kept until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Payment and transaction records are kept as long as the law requires for accounting, tax and dispute handling, even after your account is deleted.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you and to keep a record of the issue.

8.Your rights and deleting your account

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to: tell you what data we hold about you, correct or complete it, erase it, and handle a grievance about how we treat it. You may also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act.

To delete your account and data: email sughandhsharma9@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my Hando account". We will verify it is you, delete your account and active listings, and confirm within 30 days. We may keep payment records we are legally required to keep.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

9.Security

We use HTTPS, secure login through Firebase, and keep contact numbers in a protected store that is only released after a verified payment. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share your login. If we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10.Children

Hando is meant for users aged 18 and above. A person under 18 may use it only with a parent or guardian's consent and supervision. If we learn that we hold a child's data without proper consent, we will delete it. A parent or guardian can write to us to ask for this.

11.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or on the site.

12.Contact and grievances

For privacy requests or complaints, write to our Grievance Officer: see Contact & Support, or email sughandhsharma9@gmail.com.
