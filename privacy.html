Privacy Policy

This explains what information Pass-book collects, why, and how it's handled.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email & passwordTo create and log into your account (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see your raw password).
Name, school/collegeShown on your listings so buyers know who they're dealing with.
Phone/WhatsApp numberShown only to a buyer after they pay to unlock your listing.
Item photo, price, description, meetup areaShown publicly on your listing.
Payment detailsProcessed directly by Razorpay. Pass-book never sees or stores your card, UPI, or bank details — only whether a payment succeeded.

2. Who we share data with

  • Firebase (Google) — stores your account and listing data.
  • Razorpay — processes payments securely; Pass-book never handles raw payment details.
  • Vercel — hosts the website and stores listing photos.

We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share your phone number publicly — it is only revealed to a buyer who has paid to unlock a specific listing.

3. How long we keep data

A listing's public details and contact information are deleted automatically once the listing is removed — either because a buyer successfully unlocked it or because you marked it as sold. Your account information remains until you ask us to delete it.

4. Your rights

You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing sughandhsharma9@gmail.com. We'll act on verified requests within a reasonable time.

5. Security

Contact details for unsold listings are stored separately from public listing data and are only released after a verified payment. Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to Pass-book in plain text.

6. Children's privacy

Pass-book is intended for students who are old enough to independently buy and sell items and manage their own payments. It is not directed at young children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Pass-book grows. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about your data can be sent to sughandhsharma9@gmail.com.
